## Known limitations and disclosed behaviour

No `KL-NNN` is active. Disclosed behaviour (grammar-design §3, §11):

- Invalid input: a word that begins with a contextual keyword or a directive
  word may be split instead of rejected (`a modx`, `elsex`, `#iffy`).
- Inside a literal-false region a line whose first word is exactly `#if`,
  `#else` or `#end` is a directive by design, also when a non-word character
  follows (Level 1 is silent on such lines): `#if-…` opens a nested block,
  `#else:` or `#elsei` closes the region, `#end.` or `#endi` yields an `ERROR`
  that can also cover a neighbouring region line; the block still ends at the
  next `#end if`.
- Literal words where only a name is valid: `catch true`, `catch false`,
  `catch invalid`, `catch LINE_NUM`, `#const x = invalid`,
  `#const x = LINE_NUM` and `#if invalid` parse without an error, the word
  being an `identifier` (every keyword is contextual, grammar-design §4;
  BS-LEX-023). BS-ERR-003 and BS-COND-013 are enforced by their listed
  negative fixtures; these forms are not rejected.
- Recovery trees are not part of the public contract (grammar-contract §6).
  A missing block terminator can leave every later declaration inside one
  top-level `ERROR`, and zero-width nodes made only of `MISSING` tokens can
  appear.

## Downstream (V9)

Carried out in Session 03 on the local `go-treesitter` branch
`session/03-brightscript-integration` (commits `8c3ea31`, `ad232fc`), created
from the product branch `session/07-product-hardening` at `0f3e720`, which
carries the runtime bundle the integration extends (that repository's `main`,
`2f809b5`, does not). The branch is neither pushed nor merged; `main` is
unchanged.

The grammar at `daa2095` (sources identical to `ade38b3`) was converted by the
pinned converter to blob
`3d3ff236b278bdfbfffb70e1245e6c3c291e1c6aea4a15ca19834d52db829a55`, reproduced
by an independent conversion. Native C 0.25.1 records were taken for the 227
W12 inputs and the 18 edit scripts (W10 I01–I12, W09 E1–E6) of the same
sources; the test compares complete ordered trees fresh and step by step, with
tree reuse on all 34 incremental steps (of 39 edit steps; a step that follows
an error-bearing step is parsed fresh). Trees agree for all 198 error-free
inputs, for 21 of the 29 inputs with an error, and for every edit step except
the error steps of I02 and I12. The ten differing trees (BS-COND-002,
BS-COND-007 R1–R3, BS-ERR-003 ×3, BS-STMT-017 and the two edit steps) all
belong to inputs with syntax errors. `go-treesitter` KR-0005 attributes nine
of them to five mechanisms of the Go runtime's C recovery port (mechanisms 1
and 2 traced on BS-COND-002 and assigned to BS-STMT-017 and I12 by their
shape) and records the tenth (BS-ERR-003 with a member) as not isolated; it
ratchets all ten with exact signatures. One integration patch, limited to
BrightScript, makes the root start where C starts it (the first token, since
line terminators are tokens). `CGO_ENABLED=0` build, `go vet`, `go test ./...`,
the runtime invariants and the 206-grammar catalog pass; the license inventory
leaves Cooklang as the only unresolved declaration.

V9 and W14 therefore FAIL. By validation.md no pin change is proposed: ADR-0016
in `go-treesitter` is withdrawn and the branch is evidence only. A correction of
the recovery port is a cross-language change in that repository.
